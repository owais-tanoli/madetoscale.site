Missed Call Recovery: answer every caller, even when nobody can pick up

When the front desk is busy, a missed call can mean a patient who books somewhere else. This workflow texts the caller back within seconds, creates a lead, follows up and sends a booking link. Try it on a made-up dental clinic.

  1. 1Press Simulate a missed call.
  2. 2Wait for the clinic's automatic text.
  3. 3Reply in the phone or tap a quick reply, then pick a time.

Simulation of a GoHighLevel-style workflow. No real call or text is made.
