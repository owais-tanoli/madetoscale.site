Why speed matters when a new enquiry arrives

2026-11-09 · 3 min read

Someone fills in your form or sends a message because they want help now. The business that replies first usually has the advantage, simply because the person is still thinking about the problem.

A simple follow-up routine

  1. Instant reply. An automatic message confirms you got the enquiry and asks one or two quick questions.
  2. Booking link. Make the next step easy: a link to pick a time that suits them.
  3. Gentle reminders. If they do not reply, send a short follow-up after a day or two, and stop as soon as they respond or book.
  4. A personal touch. Once they reply, a real person takes over.

Common mistakes

  • Replying only during office hours, when enquiries arrive all day and night.
  • Chasing too hard, which feels pushy. Keep messages short and polite.
  • Losing the enquiry because it sits in someone's personal inbox.

Automation handles the first reply and the reminders. You handle the conversation. If you want help setting this up, get in touch.
