GoHighLevel vs HubSpot for a small business: how to choose

2026-11-09 · 5 min read

Small businesses often compare GoHighLevel (GHL) and HubSpot. Both are CRMs with marketing tools, but they suit different situations. Prices and plans change often, so check each provider's current pricing page before you decide.

Where GoHighLevel tends to fit

  • Service businesses that run on enquiries, calls, texts and bookings.
  • Automation like missed-call text back, reminders, review requests and follow-up sequences.
  • Agencies and consultants who manage several clients in one place.

Where HubSpot tends to fit

  • Larger marketing and sales teams that need deep reporting and many integrations.
  • Businesses that want a mature content, email and sales toolkit.
  • Teams that prefer a very polished, ready-made experience over customisation.

The website question

A common concern is page speed. Pages built inside any all-in-one builder can carry extra scripts, which may affect speed and search performance if the pages are heavy. Many businesses solve this by keeping a fast, separately built website and connecting its forms to the CRM. The CRM then handles the follow-up while the website stays quick.

How to decide

  1. List what you need the system to do in the next six months.
  2. Count how many people will use it.
  3. Try the real workflow on a trial, not just a demo.
  4. Think about who will set it up and maintain it.
  5. Compare the total monthly cost, including texts, calls and email usage.

There is no single winner. The better choice is the one that fits your process and that you will actually keep running. If you would like a second opinion, talk to us, and see our tool costs page for typical GHL usage charges.
