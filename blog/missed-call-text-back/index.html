Missed-call text back: how it works and why it helps

2026-11-09 · 3 min read

A customer calls while you are with another customer, on a job, or closed for the day. Nobody answers. Many people will not leave a voicemail. They simply call the next business on the list.

How it works

  1. A call to your business number goes unanswered.
  2. Within seconds, the caller gets a short text from the same number, for example: "Sorry we missed you. How can we help? You can also book here."
  3. The text includes a booking link, and any reply lands in your inbox with the full history.
  4. The caller is saved as a contact, so you can follow up properly instead of guessing who rang.

Why it helps

It does not replace answering the phone. It covers the moments you cannot, so a missed call becomes a conversation instead of a lost customer. Most people are happier to reply to a text than to call back.

What you need

  • A business phone number that can send texts.
  • A short, friendly message in your own tone.
  • A booking link or a clear question to move the conversation forward.
  • A one-time carrier registration for texting in some countries. We handle this during setup.
